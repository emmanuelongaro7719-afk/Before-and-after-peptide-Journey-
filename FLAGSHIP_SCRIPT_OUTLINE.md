# Flagship Video — Beat-by-Beat Script Outline

**Working title:** "I Fought My Weight for 30 Years. Here's How It Finally Ended."
**Target length:** 12–18 minutes
**Structure:** Cold open → Act 1 (the climb) → Act 2 (the rollercoaster) → Act 3 (the turn) → Resolution → CTA

---

## Cold Open (0:00–0:45) — earn the next 15 minutes

Start at the emotional peak, not the beginning.

> "In December 1993, I stepped off a plane from France weighing 178 pounds. Within a few years I was over 200. At my worst, I was over 300. I spent thirty years losing weight and gaining it back — every diet, every time. Today I weigh 192 pounds at 12.5% body fat. This is the whole story: the thirty years of failure, and the two medications that ended it."

- Show the before/after photos in the first 10 seconds. Don't save them for the end — the transformation is why they clicked; the *story* is why they'll stay.
- On-screen text: **178 → 300+ → 192**

## Act 1 — The Climb (0:45–4:00)

- **France, before America:** what eating looked like — portions, meal structure, walking. "I was never a big guy in France."
- **December 1993:** why you came, first impressions. Small sensory details land here (first American supermarket, portion sizes, drive-everywhere culture).
- **The quiet gain:** 178 → 200+ in a few years. Key beat: *it didn't feel like anything was wrong while it was happening.*
- Photos from the era throughout.

## Act 2 — The Rollercoaster (4:00–8:00)

This is the section every yo-yo dieter will feel in their chest.

- Rapid-fire montage of the diets across three decades — name them (low-fat, Atkins, Weight Watchers, keto, fasting... whatever your actual list is). For 2–3 of them: how much you lost, how long it lasted, how the regain started.
- **The pattern:** the loss was always temporary, the regain always came, and each cycle ended a little heavier. The rollercoaster only went up.
- **Rock bottom beat:** crossing 300. Pick ONE specific concrete moment — the scale, a doctor's sentence, a seatbelt extender, a photo you hated. Specific beats general, every time.
- **The reframe (thesis of the video):** "For thirty years I thought I was failing the diets. It took me thirty years to understand the diets were failing me — I wasn't fighting a discipline problem, I was fighting my own biology, and biology doesn't lose to willpower."

## Act 3 — The Turn (8:00–12:00)

- How you first heard about GLP-1 medications, and your honest skepticism ("after 30 years, why would THIS work?").
- **Starting tirzepatide:** the first weeks. The "food noise going silent" moment deserves a full 60 seconds — it's the single most powerful concept in this niche and non-users have never heard of it.
- The weight coming off — but this time *different*: no white-knuckling, no countdown to the regain.
- **The retatrutide chapter:** why the change, and the shift from "losing weight" to "building the body" — protein, lifting, protecting muscle. Clearly state on camera that retatrutide is investigational and not FDA-approved, and that this is your experience, not a recommendation.
- Honest costs: side effects you had, money, the adjustment period. Credibility lives here.

## Resolution (12:00–14:00)

- Today: 192 lbs, 12.5% body fat. What daily life is like now — the small things (clothes, stairs, photos, energy, how doctors talk to you).
- Callback to the cold open: the guy who stepped off that plane in 1993.
- **The honest closer:** this isn't "peptides fixed everything" — it's "the medication ended a biological war so I could finally do the work that never stuck before."

## CTA (14:00–end)

- "I'm going to break down every part of this — the month-by-month, the muscle, the mistakes, the maintenance — in the next videos. Subscribe if you've ever lost the same 50 pounds twice."
- Point to the next video (Week 2: "Every diet I tried in 30 years").
