# Before & After: A Peptide Journey

YouTube content plan for a weight loss transformation channel.

**The story:** A 30+ year battle with weight — from arriving in the US from France in December 1993 at 5'7" and 178 lbs, climbing past 300 lbs through decades of yo-yo dieting, and finally reaching **192 lbs at 12.5% body fat** with the help of tirzepatide and retatrutide.
